Seven Key Concepts

These terms provide a foundation for understanding web and information security.

1. Authentication

Verifying that a user or system is who it claims to be.

2. Authorization

Deciding which resources and actions an authenticated user may access.

3. Encryption

Transforming readable information into protected ciphertext using cryptographic keys.

4. Vulnerability

A weakness that could be exploited to affect a system's security.

5. Threat

A potential event or actor that could cause harm to a system or its data.

6. Multi-Factor Authentication

Requiring more than one distinct type of evidence to verify identity.

7. Least Privilege

Granting only the permissions necessary for a specific task.
