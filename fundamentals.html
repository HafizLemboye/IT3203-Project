Cybersecurity Fundamentals

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications, and data against unauthorized access, disruption, or damage. Security planning considers both technical controls and human behavior.

Confidentiality

Restrict information to authorized people. Access controls and encryption help protect private data.

Integrity

Keep information accurate and prevent unauthorized changes. Checksums and audit logs can help detect tampering.

Availability

Keep services accessible when needed. Backups, redundancy, and incident response support availability.

Common Threats

  • Phishing: deceptive messages intended to obtain information or access.
  • Malware: harmful software that can steal data or disrupt operations.
  • Credential attacks: attempts to compromise accounts using stolen or guessed passwords.
  • Denial of service: overwhelming a service so legitimate users cannot access it.
